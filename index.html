01 — Mission

Technology should feel clear, useful, and human.

I'm not interested in technology for its own sake. I'm interested in what it makes possible for the person on the other side of the screen.

So I build the way I was trained to design: start with the problem, remove everything that isn't the answer, and make what's left feel obvious.

02 — Story

I came to code through design.

Design

I started in design. It taught me to think about people first: how things communicate, how they should feel, why a thing works before it's ever built.

Engineering

I got curious about how products are actually made — and that curiosity moved me into frontend, then full-stack. React, React Native, TypeScript, Node.js. I stopped handing off ideas and started shipping them.

Leadership

Responsibility arrived the way it usually does: quietly. Helping other developers. Owning technical decisions. Improving how the team works. Today I lead technically — sitting between product needs, user experience, and engineering, and making sure those three stay in the same conversation.

What's next

Lately I've been building with AI. Not as a productivity trick, but as a new material: a way to make products that weren't possible two years ago.

“I like solving real problems, simplifying complexity, and building things that are useful, scalable, and thoughtfully designed.”
