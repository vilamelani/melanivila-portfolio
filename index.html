02 — Story

I came to code through design.

Design

I started in design. It taught me to think about people first: how things communicate, how they should feel, why a thing works before it's ever built.

Engineering

I got curious about how products are actually made, and that moved me into front-end, then full stack. React, React Native, TypeScript, Node.js. I stopped handing off ideas and started shipping them.

Teams

Over time I took on more responsibility: helping other developers, making technical decisions, and improving how the team works. Today I work in product teams, from the interface to the backend.

What's next

Lately I've been building with AI, to make products that weren't practical a couple of years ago.

“I like solving real problems, simplifying complexity, and building things that are useful and easy to maintain.”
